    Extra hard scatterings, small systems and minimum bias¶

    Without extra options, every event of the jet production has exactly one hard scattering: the trigger. A nucleus–nucleus collision has many more, N_coll × σ(p̂T > p₀)/σ_NN of them, and at the LHC they matter: their fragments are the jet background a Pb+Pb event otherwise lacks (the fake jets, the B of Z+jet, the hard tail of the underlying event), and their wakes change the bulk (Nch, [pT], vₙ). run_prod_jet.py --extra-hard adds these extras above a threshold p₀ to every event. The same option, without a trigger, gives minimum-bias events, and two new inputs run O+O and Ne+Ne at 5.36 TeV. Everything on Au+Au jets applies; this page adds what is new. The analysis side is on Bulk and flow and in the Hadrons page.

    Work in progress

    The analysis scripts and notebooks for these files (bulk and flow, v₂(p_T) against data) are work in progress and need further vetting; they are meant mainly as examples of how to use the simulation outputs. See the note on the analysis section.

    Needs the current code

    The option needs X-SCAPE with PythiaGun <extraHard> and the current js-contrib. Check an image first: python run_prod_jet.py --help must list --extra-hard. On a Mac, the xscape wheel has it from 0.2.0. A 0.1.0 environment stops with User XML tag <extraHard> is unrecognized, also for the XMLs that carry the block switched off (AuAu_MCGlauber_MUSIC_0_10_jet.xml, PbPb_0_10_jet.xml, PbPb_0_30_ZJet.xml): install the current release next to it.

    How many extras¶

    Per binary collision (LO Pythia 8.3, HardQCD, ISR on, MPI and FSR off):

    √s_NN σ(p̂T > 10 GeV) σ(p̂T > 15 GeV) extras per binary collision, p₀ = 10 / 15 GeV
    200 GeV 0.0058 mb 0.00054 mb 1.4·10⁻⁴ / 1.3·10⁻⁵
    5.02 TeV 2.31 mb 0.52 mb 0.034 / 0.0077
    5.36 TeV 2.54 mb 0.58 mb 0.037 / 0.0085

    Per event:

    system ⟨N_coll⟩ p₀ (XML default) extras per event
    Au+Au 200 GeV, 0–10 % ~960 10 GeV ~0.13 (at RHIC they barely matter)
    Pb+Pb 5.02 TeV, 0–10 % ~1,800 15 GeV ~15 (~66 at 10 GeV)
    Pb+Pb 5.02 TeV, 0–30 % 1,030 15 GeV ~8 (~35 at 10 GeV)
    O+O 5.36 TeV, 0–10 % 42 10 GeV ~1.5
    Ne+Ne 5.36 TeV, 0–10 % 51 10 GeV ~1.9

    Each extra hands over ~4.5–4.8 partons (its two hard partons and the ISR partons above 2 GeV), Σp_T ~40–60 GeV. The physics, and what the model leaves out, is in The physics.

    How an event is made¶

    IS (3dMCGlauber) -> Hard (PythiaGun: the trigger, then N ~ Poisson(N_coll sigma/sigma_NN) extras)
   -> MUSIC_1                    background leg: the strings alone           -> bulk_bg
   -> Liquefier + Eloss          Matter + LBT shower the trigger and the extras -> droplets
   -> MUSIC_2                    the same strings + all droplets             -> bulk_jet
   -> hadronization              trigger's partons -> jet_frag, the extras' -> extra_frag
    • Each extra is one event of a Pythia instance of its own (HardQCD above p₀), at a binary collision drawn from the background's collisions; its partons are handed over after the trigger's, labelled with their scattering (1, 2, …).
    • The trigger is untouched. Everything random the extras do (their Pythia, the Poisson draw, the vertices, their shower engines, their colour tags and hadronization seeds) runs on streams of their own. With the same seed, a job with extras has the same trigger showers, jet_frag and bulk_bg as a job without, bit for bit, in every event; only bulk_jet (their wakes) and the new records differ. Running the same seeds twice, with and without --extra-hard, is a paired measurement of what the extras do.
    • With --reuse every jet event draws its own extras from its background's N_coll and binary collisions.
    • Their energy is not subtracted from the strings, as for the trigger.

    Running it¶

    option of run_prod_jet.py
    --extra-hard switch the extras on. Off (default): the files are bit-identical to before
    --extra-pthat-min P0 their threshold [GeV]; default: the XML's <extraHard><pTHatMin>
    --extra-n N exactly N extras per event instead of Poisson (tests)
    --extra-warmup N Pythia events for their cross section at Init (default 20,000, ~1 s at the LHC)
    --extra-hadronize {groups,per_scattering} with --hadrons-only: all extras' showers in one Colorless call (default), or one call per scattering, with each hadron's scattering kept
    --hard none no trigger: the minimum-bias event, the bulk and its extras (N may be 0), weight 1. Needs --extra-hard; refuses --pthat-*, --parton-ymax, --trigger. No jet_frag

    Everything else works as without extras: --reuse, --pthat-bins, --trigger Z, --hadrons-only, --write-particlize, --correlated, run_jobs.sh and the container launchers. run_prod_vacuum.py (pp) ignores the extras: pp has one collision and Pythia's own MPI.

    cd prod_AuAu_0_10_jet                            # in your copy of the examples
# Au+Au 0-10 % with extras above 10 GeV (the XML's)
python run_prod_jet.py --events 10 --seed 1 --extra-hard --hadrons-only

# Pb+Pb 0-30 % Z+jet with extras above 15 GeV
python run_prod_jet.py --events 24 --seed 1 --user-xml ../config/PbPb_0_30_ZJet.xml \
    --grid ../config/grid_PbPb.yaml --pthat-bins 20-30,30-35,35-45,45-55,55-70,70-200 \
    --jets-per-bin 4 --lepton-ptmin 20 --lepton-etamax 2.5 --extra-hard --hadrons-only

# a two-stage campaign: particlize files, then hadronize.py (which handles the extras itself)
python run_prod_jet.py --events 10 --seed 1 --extra-hard --write-particlize both --particlize-only
python hadronize.py out/AuAu_0_10_jet_seed0001_particlize.h5 --oversample 20 --n-frag 10

    The job prints the extras' settings once, and their number per event:

      extras   pTHat > 15 GeV: sigma = 0.524 +- 0.002 mb (20000 warm-up events, Pythia seed 234669607), sigma_NN 67.57 mb, N ~ Poisson(N_coll sigma/sigma_NN)
prod_AuAu_0_10_jet: event 1/1 done in 40.5 s, ..., N_coll 1294, ..., 10 extra scattering(s) (mu 10.04, 3342 GeV)

    The energy in that line includes the extras' forward ISR partons, which carry most of it; the summed p_T (extra_pt in the files) is the better measure of what they bring to mid-rapidity.

    Choosing p₀¶

    Lower thresholds put more of the hard tail into the bulk and cost more. Below ~10 GeV the extras' energy becomes a sizeable part of what the strings already carry; that needs minijets with energy subtraction and a retuned bulk, which this option does not do. The production XMLs set 10 GeV for Au+Au, O+O and Ne+Ne, 15 GeV for Pb+Pb.

    Cost on the GB10 (Pb+Pb 0–10 %, 3 events, the same backgrounds, one job alone, --hadrons-only; hadronization ~5 s per event either way):

    extras per event droplets s per event peak memory
    extras off — 45 32.1 18.98 GB
    p₀ = 20 GeV ~5 195 34.5 (+8 %) 19.04 GB
    p₀ = 15 GeV (Pb+Pb default) ~15 464 36.4 (+13 %) 19.08 GB
    p₀ = 10 GeV ~66 1,300 45.6 (+42 %) 19.16 GB

    The time grows with the showers and with MUSIC_2's jet source (all cells × the active droplets, on the CPU); the warm-up for σ adds ~1 s per job. Memory hardly changes.

    The XML settings¶

    The driver writes the settings into <Hard><PythiaGun><extraHard> of the job XML; set them there yourself to change a default for a whole campaign. The defaults are in X-SCAPE's config/jetscape_main.xml.

    tag default
    <on> 0 1: the extras on (--extra-hard)
    <noTrigger> 0 1: no triggering scattering, the extras alone (--hard none). Refuses <pTHatBins>, <partonYMax>, <trigger>
    <pTHatMin> 15 their p̂T threshold [GeV] (--extra-pthat-min). The production XMLs set their system's: 10 (AuAu_MCGlauber_MUSIC_0_10_jet.xml, OO_5360_jet.xml, NeNe_5360_jet.xml), 15 (PbPb_0_10_jet.xml, PbPb_0_30_ZJet.xml)
    <sigmaNN> empty σ_NN^inel [mb]; empty: 3dMCGlauber's parametrization at <eCM>, the one its N_coll is made with (40.8 mb at 200 GeV, 67.6 at 5.02 TeV, 68.3 at 5.36 TeV)
    <warmup> 20000 Pythia events at Init for σ(p̂T > p₀) (--extra-warmup); kept fixed for the job
    <fixedN> −1 ≥ 0: exactly that many per event (--extra-n, tests); < 0: Poisson
    <LinesToRead> empty more Pythia settings for the extras' instance only (not the trigger's <LinesToRead>, which may hold a Z process)

    The extras' Pythia runs HardQCD:all = on with PhaseSpace:pTHatMin = p₀, ISR on, MPI off, FSR as <FSR_on> (off in production: Matter showers), hadron level off, and the trigger's <eCM> and <softMomentumCutoff>.

    Minimum bias¶

    --hard none --extra-hard makes events without a trigger: the bulk and its extras, N of them from Poisson (N may be 0), every event with weight 1. An event with N = 0 has no hard parton: the energy loss is skipped, MUSIC_2 still runs (restarted from MUSIC_1's checkpoint, so it is cheap), and bulk_jet differs from bulk_bg only by sampling. bulk_jet − bulk_bg is then the extras' wakes. These are the events for the bulk observables of Bulk and flow; use correlated legs and several samples so that the paired differences are small-noise:

    TAG_PREFIX=PbPb_0_10_seed ./run_jobs.sh -j 4 --campaign mb_x 20 30 0 out_mb_x \
    --user-xml ../config/PbPb_0_10_jet.xml --grid ../config/grid_PbPb.yaml \
    --hard none --extra-hard --hadrons-only --oversample 10 --correlated

    For the 0–10 % XMLs this is minimum bias within 0–10 %; set b_max to 20 in a copy of the XML for all impact parameters. Scale the threshold with --extra-pthat-min 20, 15, 10 on the same seeds (the backgrounds are the same) to see the dependence on p₀.

    O+O and Ne+Ne at 5.36 TeV¶

    example/config/OO_5360_jet.xml and NeNe_5360_jet.xml (in the image's example/config/) are copies of PbPb_0_10_jet.xml for the LHC's light-ion run:

    • 3dMCGlauber's ¹⁶O and PGCM ²⁰Ne configurations (Ne20 is 3dMCGlauber's name for neon), √s_NN = 5.36 TeV, MUSIC's transverse grid ±12 fm, the output grid grid_oo.yaml;
    • 0–10 %: b < 2.09 fm (O+O) and 2.41 fm (Ne+Ne), the 10 % quantile of b in 80,000 minimum-bias 3dMCGlauber events, ⟨N_coll⟩ 42 / 51; set b_max to 20 for minimum bias;
    • <extraHard><pTHatMin> 10 GeV.

    Not refitted for the LHC or for small systems

    The bulk settings are the Pb+Pb file's: the RHIC best fit of the JETSCAPE 3D calibration (arXiv:2601.17234), without the paper's Pb+Pb refit and without an afterburner (The physics). Two test events give dN_ch/dη(|η| < 0.5) ≈ 136 (O+O) and 131 (Ne+Ne), ⟨pT⟩ ≈ 0.59 / 0.65 GeV. Compare dN_ch/dη and v₂{2} with ATLAS (arXiv:2606.20257) and ALICE (arXiv:2604.07184) before quoting numbers. The 0–10 % edges are geometric, not multiplicity classes.

    # 0-10 % with a jet trigger and extras
python run_prod_jet.py --events 10 --seed 1 --user-xml ../config/OO_5360_jet.xml \
    --grid ../config/grid_oo.yaml --extra-hard --hadrons-only --out OO_5360_seed0001.h5
python run_prod_jet.py --events 10 --seed 1 --user-xml ../config/NeNe_5360_jet.xml \
    --grid ../config/grid_oo.yaml --extra-hard --hadrons-only --out NeNe_5360_seed0001.h5

# minimum-bias campaigns, correlated legs, 10 samples per event
TAG_PREFIX=OO_5360_seed ./run_jobs.sh --campaign mb1 20 30 1 out_oo_mb1 \
    --user-xml ../config/OO_5360_jet.xml --grid ../config/grid_oo.yaml \
    --hard none --extra-hard --hadrons-only --oversample 10 --correlated
                                                  # -> OO_5360_mb1_seed0001.* ...
TAG_PREFIX=NeNe_5360_seed ./run_jobs.sh --campaign mb1 20 30 1 out_nene_mb1 \
    --user-xml ../config/NeNe_5360_jet.xml --grid ../config/grid_oo.yaml \
    --hard none --extra-hard --hadrons-only --oversample 10 --correlated

    These run through the jet production's driver, so they are not the O+O with ISR production at 200 GeV: no initial-state shower module, the two-leg pair, and the Au+Au jet production's files.

    Name the files

    The driver's default name is AuAu_0_10_jet[_<campaign>]_seed<NNNN>, whatever the XML, so without a name the O+O, Ne+Ne and Pb+Pb runs above are all written as AuAu_0_10_jet_*. Use --out for a single job and TAG_PREFIX for a campaign through run_jobs.sh (it must end in _seed; one prefix per OUTDIR). launch_2gpu.sh and slurm_prod_array.sh pass TAG_PREFIX into the container.

    What the files hold¶

    • <stem>_hadrons_extra_frag.h5: the extras' fragments, with the layout of jet_frag (unit = event, --n-frag samples), plus scatterings/ and the extras' initiators/. jet_frag stays the trigger's alone and is absent with --hard none.
    • scatterings/ (pair, particlize and extra_frag files): one row per scattering, the trigger's first: scatter, pthat, code, x, y, n_partons, E, pt.
    • Per event (diag/, events/, units/): n_extra, extra_mu (= N_coll σ/σ_NN), extra_E, extra_pt.
    • Attributes and the .json: the threshold, σ and its error, σ_NN, the warm-up and the seeds of the extras (extra_*, prod_extra_*).

    Files without extras have none of these; their layout is unchanged. Full layout: What a production writes.
